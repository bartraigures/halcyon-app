Support

Halcyon for iPhone and iPad

If something isn't working, or works in a way you didn't expect, I'd like to know. The quickest way to reach me is to open an issue here. Say what you did, what happened, and which device and iOS version you're on. A screenshot helps a lot.

You need a free GitHub account to do that. If you'd rather not, leave a review on the App Store and I'll see it there.

Where are my files?

In the Files app under iCloud Drive › Halcyon. Each project is a .md file and the Inbox is Inbox.md. If iCloud Drive is turned off, they're in On My iPhone (or iPad) › Halcyon instead.

I edited a file somewhere else and the app hasn't caught up

iCloud can take a little while to bring changes across, especially if the other device has been asleep. Pull down on any list to refresh. If the file still looks old, open it in Files once, which makes iCloud download it.

Siri adds things to Reminders instead

Say "Add to Halcyon", or "Add to my to do list in Halcyon". If Siri still picks Reminders, make a shortcut of your own called "Add to my to do list" that runs Halcyon's Add to Inbox action. Your own shortcut always wins.

Reminders don't arrive on my Watch or Mac

Turn on the Apple Reminders link in Halcyon's Settings. Each task with a reminder then gets a matching one in a "Halcyon" list in Reminders, and those alerts go everywhere Reminders does. Ticking it off there ticks the task too.

Quick Add doesn't understand my dates

The full natural language reading needs a device with Apple Intelligence switched on. Without it Halcyon falls back to a simpler reader that handles things like "tomorrow 3pm" and "next Friday", but not the longer, chattier phrases.

Something looks duplicated or there's a conflict

Halcyon never deletes anything it can't merge. Conflicting versions are kept in the hidden .halcyon/conflicts folder, and Conflicts appears in the sidebar so you can choose which version to keep, or keep both.
