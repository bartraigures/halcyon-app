Halcyon

To do lists in plain Markdown, for iPhone and iPad

I built Halcyon because I wanted a proper task manager that didn't lock my lists away in somebody else's database.

Every project is a plain Markdown file in your iCloud Drive. Open them in Files, Obsidian or any text editor, change things there, and Halcyon picks it up. If you stop using the app one day, your lists are still there, exactly as you left them.

It's built around Getting Things Done: an Inbox for capture, Next Actions by @context, Waiting For, Someday/Maybe and a Weekly Review. There are widgets, Siri phrases, a share sheet extension, reminders that can copy into Apple Reminders, and natural language dates worked out on the phone itself.

No account, no subscription, and no data collected.
