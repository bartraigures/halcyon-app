Privacy

Halcyon for iPhone and iPad. Last updated 13 November 2026.

The short version: Halcyon doesn't collect anything. I can't see your tasks, and neither can anyone else.

Your tasks

Your lists are Markdown files in your own iCloud Drive, or on the device if iCloud Drive is off. They sync through Apple's iCloud under your Apple Account, the same as any other file you keep there. They never pass through a server of mine, because there isn't one.

No accounts, analytics or ads

There's no sign-in, no analytics, no crash reporting service, no advertising and no third-party code that phones home. The app doesn't make network requests of its own.

Natural language in Quick Add

When you type something like "call Bob next Thursday afternoon", the reading is done on the device using Apple's on-device models. The text isn't sent anywhere.

Reminders and notifications

If you switch on the Apple Reminders link, Halcyon asks for access to Reminders and keeps a list called "Halcyon" there. It only reads and writes that list. Notifications are scheduled on the device.

Siri, widgets and Spotlight

Siri requests go through Apple as they do for any app, and Halcyon only receives the text of the task. Widgets read a small summary file kept on the device. Spotlight indexes your open task titles on the device so you can search for them.

Children

Halcyon doesn't collect data from anyone, children included.

Changes and contact

If this ever changes I'll update this page and the date at the top before the app does anything different. Questions are welcome via the support page.
